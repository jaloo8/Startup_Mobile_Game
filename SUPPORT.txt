ABOUT VENTURE
============================================================

Venture is a premium mobile startup simulation game where
you build a tech company from a garage to IPO. Make strategic
decisions about hiring, product development, fundraising,
and operations across 20-40 quarters of gameplay.

  Platform: iOS and Android
  Price: Free trial (8 quarters), then $2.99 one-time unlock
  Age rating: 13+
  Internet required: No (offline single-player)
  Accounts required: No (no login or registration needed)

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
GETTING STARTED
------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How do I start playing?

A: Download Venture from the App Store or Google Play. Tap
"New Game" on the main menu, choose your company type (SaaS,
Consumer App, Marketplace, or Dev Tools), select a difficulty,
and start building your startup. A short tutorial will guide
you through the basics on your first run.


Q: Is the game free?

A: Venture offers a free trial covering the first 8 quarters
(2 in-game years) of gameplay. After that, a one-time
purchase of $2.99 unlocks the full game permanently. There
are no subscriptions, no additional purchases, and no ads.


Q: What age is the game for?

A: Venture is intended for players aged 13 and older. The
game involves business strategy concepts like hiring,
fundraising, and financial management.

------------------------------------------------------------
PURCHASES AND PAYMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How do I unlock the full game?

A: When you reach the end of the free trial (Year 2,
Quarter 4), you will see a screen offering the full game
unlock for $2.99. Tap "Unlock Full Game" to purchase through
your device's app store (Apple App Store or Google Play).


Q: I already purchased the game but it is showing as locked.
   How do I fix this?

A: Go to the Game's Settings screen or the paywall screen
and tap "Restore Purchase." Make sure you are signed into
the same Apple ID or Google account you used for the original
purchase. If the issue persists, try the following:

  1. Ensure you have an internet connection
  2. Sign out and back into your app store account
  3. Restart the Game
  4. Restart your device

If none of these steps work, contact us at [YOUR_EMAIL] with
your purchase receipt (available in your app store purchase
history).


Q: Can I get a refund?

A: We do not process refunds directly. Refunds are handled
by the platform where you made your purchase:

  Apple App Store:
  https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204084

  Google Play Store:
  https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637


Q: Can I transfer my purchase between iOS and Android?

A: No. Purchases made on the Apple App Store cannot be
transferred to Google Play, and vice versa. Each platform
manages purchases independently.

------------------------------------------------------------
SAVE DATA AND GAME PROGRESS
------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Where is my game data stored?

A: All save data is stored locally on your device. We do not
store your game data on any server or cloud service.


Q: How do I back up my save data?

A: Use your device's built-in backup features:
  - iOS: Enable iCloud backup or connect to a computer
    and back up through Finder/iTunes
  - Android: Enable Google backup in your device settings


Q: I lost my save data. Can you recover it?

A: Unfortunately, no. Because all data is stored locally on
your device, we cannot access or recover your save data. The
Game does include automatic backup and recovery for save file
corruption, but if the app is uninstalled or the device is
reset, the data cannot be recovered.


Q: How many save slots do I have?

A: Free trial users have 2 save slots. Premium users (after
purchasing the full game) have 5 save slots.

------------------------------------------------------------
GAMEPLAY
------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How long is a full game?

A: A typical game runs 20-40 quarters (5-10 in-game startup
years). Each quarter takes roughly 5-15 minutes to play,
so a full game takes several hours spread across multiple
sessions.


Q: What are the different company types?

A: You can build one of four types of startup:
  - SaaS (Software as a Service): B2B subscription software
  - Consumer App: Consumer-facing mobile/web application
  - Marketplace: Two-sided marketplace platform
  - Dev Tools: Developer-focused tools and APIs

Each type has unique revenue models, growth dynamics, and
strategic considerations.


Q: How do I win?

A: You can achieve victory by reaching a sustainable scale
with 6 consecutive profitable quarters after Year 5, meeting
type-specific growth thresholds. You can also continue
playing after victory in sandbox mode.


Q: The game seems too hard / too easy. What can I do?

A: Venture offers three difficulty levels:
  - Sandbox: Relaxed mode for learning the mechanics
  - Normal: Balanced challenge
  - Hardcore: Demanding survival mode

You can also try different company types and niches, which
each present unique strategic challenges.

------------------------------------------------------------
PRIVACY AND DATA
------------------------------------------------------------

Q: What data does the game collect?

A: For premium users (paid): NONE. No data is collected.

For free trial users: Anonymous gameplay analytics only,
with your consent and after age confirmation (13+). This
includes things like how far you get in the game, which
features you use, and basic device info. No personal
information is ever collected. You can disable analytics
anytime in Settings. See our full Privacy Policy for details.


Q: Does the game have ads?

A: No. Venture is a premium game with no advertisements.


Q: Does the game track me across other apps?

A: No. We do not engage in any cross-app or cross-site
tracking. We do not use advertising identifiers.


Q: How do I disable analytics?

A: Go to Settings from the main menu and toggle off
"Share anonymous gameplay data." Analytics can also be
permanently disabled by purchasing the full game.

TECHNICAL ISSUES
------------------------------------------------------------

Q: The game is crashing. What should I do?

A: Try the following steps:
  1. Close the Game completely and reopen it
  2. Restart your device
  3. Ensure your device's operating system is up to date
  4. Ensure you have the latest version of Venture installed
  5. If the issue persists, contact us at [YOUR_EMAIL]
     with your device model, OS version, and a description
     of when the crash occurs


Q: The game runs slowly on my device. What can I do?

A: Venture is designed to run well on a wide range of
devices. If you experience performance issues:
  1. Close other apps running in the background
  2. Restart your device
  3. Make sure you are running the latest version of the Game
  4. Contact us if the issue persists


Q: The game is not available in my country.

A: Venture is intended to be available worldwide. If you
cannot find it in your local app store, it may be due to
regional availability restrictions set by the platform.
Please contact us and we will look into it.
