# Roguelike GM — Monetization Plan

## Status

**Draft.** Decide before or during prototype; avoid retrofitting monetization later.

## Options to consider

### Premium (buy once)

- **Pros:** Simple, no IAP complexity, trusted by “premium mobile” audience.
- **Cons:** Lower ceiling; need strong word-of-mouth or featuring.

### Free-to-play (F2P)

- **Cosmetic IAP only** — Skins, card backs, stadium themes. Keeps gameplay fair.
- **Optional “season pass” / battle pass** — Cosmetics + maybe convenience (e.g. extra run slot). No pay-to-win.
- **Ads (optional)** — Rewarded only (e.g. “watch ad for one mulligan this run”). Interstitials risk hurting “one more run” feel.

### Hybrid

- Free download, optional tip / “supporter” one-time IAP that unlocks cosmetics or removes ads. Premium feel with a low barrier.

## Recommendation for solo dev

Start **premium** or **free + cosmetic IAP only**. Avoid pay-to-win and intrusive ads so the core loop stays clean. Revisit after you have a playable prototype and a sense of retention.

## Notes

- Apple: 30% (15% for small business); consider pricing and paywall placement.
- TestFlight and soft launch are good places to test price sensitivity and IAP interest.
