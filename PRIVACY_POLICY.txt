3. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT
============================================================

3.1 ANONYMOUS ANALYTICS DATA

When analytics are enabled, we collect anonymous, non-personally
identifiable gameplay telemetry:

  - Session data: session start/end timestamps, session duration
  - Progression data: quarter/year reached, game outcome
  - Feature usage: screen visits, button interactions
  - Economy data: aggregate in-game cash flow patterns
  - Technical errors: error type and message for bug fixing
  - Device metadata: OS, OS version, device model, screen
    resolution, app version

3.2 DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

We explicitly DO NOT collect:

  - No personal identifiers (names, usernames, emails, phone
    numbers, mailing addresses)
  - No account data (no accounts or logins required)
  - No financial data (purchases handled by app stores)
  - No location data (no GPS, IP geolocation, cell tower)
  - No device contents (no photos, contacts, files, camera,
    microphone, or sensors)
  - No advertising identifiers (no IDFA or GAID for analytics)
  - No social media data
  - No cross-app tracking
  - No biometric data
  - No health data

3.3 PLATFORM APP STORES

Downloads and purchases through Apple App Store or Google Play
are subject to their respective privacy policies:
  - Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  - Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.4 AUTOMATICALLY COLLECTED TECHNICAL DATA

GameAnalytics (our analytics processor) automatically collects
when analytics are enabled:
  - A randomly generated installation identifier (not linked to
    your real identity or any personal account)
  - Operating system name and version
  - Device model, app version, language/locale

This data is used solely for analytics aggregation and cannot
identify you personally.

============================================================

6.1 ANALYTICS OPT-OUT (ALL USERS)

Disable analytics at any time:
  1. Open the Game
  2. Go to Settings (from the main menu)
  3. Toggle "Share anonymous gameplay data" off

When disabled:
  - No new data is collected or transmitted
  - The Game functions identically — nothing is withheld
  - Previously collected data follows retention policy (Sec. 8)

6.2 DEFAULT SETTINGS

  Free-to-Play: Analytics enabled after age confirmation (13+).
  Players must confirm age on first launch, are informed about
  data collection, and can disable anytime.

  Premium: Analytics NOT available. No data is collected from
  premium users under any circumstances.

6.2a AGE VERIFICATION (FREE-TO-PLAY)

  Before any analytics data is collected, F2P players are asked
  to confirm they are 13 years of age or older. If a player
