3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
============================================================

3.1 LOCAL SAVE DATA (STORED ON YOUR DEVICE ONLY)

The Game stores your game progress, settings, and
preferences locally on your device within the application
sandbox. This data includes:

  - Game save files (company state, employees, financials,
    game progress, quarter history)
  - User preferences (difficulty, sound settings, tutorial
    completion status)
  - Analytics consent preference
  - Age confirmation status
  - Premium unlock status

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to
any server, cloud service, or third party. It is accessible
only to the Game application and is deleted when you
uninstall the Game or clear its app data.

3.2 ANONYMOUS DIAGNOSTIC AND PRODUCT IMPROVEMENT DATA
    (FREE TRIAL ONLY, WITH CONSENT)

During the free trial period, and only after you confirm
you are 13 or older and consent to data collection, we
collect anonymous, non-personally-identifiable diagnostic
telemetry. This data is used exclusively for debugging,
crash resolution, and product improvement. It contains no
personal information whatsoever — we cannot identify,
contact, or profile any individual player from this data.

  Session diagnostics
    Examples: Session start/end timestamps, session duration
    Purpose: Detect crashes, freezes, and performance
    bottlenecks

  Progression diagnostics
    Examples: Quarter/year reached, game outcome (win/loss
    and reason)
    Purpose: Identify difficulty spikes, softlocks, and
    balance issues

  Feature diagnostics
    Examples: Screen visits, button interactions
    Purpose: Find broken UI elements and unused features

  Economy diagnostics
    Examples: Aggregate in-game cash flow patterns
    Purpose: Detect balance exploits and economy-breaking
    bugs

  Technical errors
    Examples: Error type, severity, and message (no stack
    traces, no file paths, no device-specific identifiers)
    Purpose: Fix crashes and bugs

  Device compatibility
    Examples: OS name, OS version, device model family,
    screen resolution, app version, language/locale
    Purpose: Ensure the game runs correctly across devices

This data is anonymous. It cannot be linked to you, your
Apple ID, your Google account, your phone number, your
email, your IP address, or any other identifier. We do not
and cannot reconstruct who you are from this data.

Premium users (those who have purchased the full game) have
zero analytics collected, automatically and permanently.
Purchasing the game immediately disables all diagnostic data
collection.

3.3 INSTALLATION IDENTIFIER

When diagnostics are enabled, GameAnalytics (our analytics
processor) generates a random installation identifier. This
identifier:

  - Is randomly generated and unique to your app
    installation
  - Is NOT linked to your Apple ID, Google account, real
    identity, or any personal account
  - Is NOT an advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID) and
    cannot be used for ad targeting or cross-app tracking
  - Cannot be used to identify you personally
  - Is deleted when raw analytics events expire (90 days)

3.4 DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT — COMPREHENSIVE LIST

We explicitly do not collect, process, store, transmit, or
have access to:

  - No personal identifiers — no real names, usernames,
    email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses
  - No account data — the Game does not require, support,
    or create user accounts, logins, or registrations of
    any kind
  - No financial or payment data — all purchases are
    handled entirely by the platform app stores
    (Apple/Google); we never see, process, store, or have
    access to payment card numbers, bank accounts, billing
    addresses, or transaction details
  - No location data — no GPS, IP-based geolocation, cell
    tower data, Wi-Fi triangulation, or any other location
    technology
  - No IP addresses — we do not log, store, or process
    your IP address
  - No device contents — no access to photos, contacts,
    files, calendar, camera, microphone, clipboard, or
    device sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, etc.)
  - No advertising identifiers — no IDFA (iOS), no GAID
    (Android), no fingerprinting
  - No social media data or social graph information
  - No cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind
  - No biometric data — no fingerprints, face scans, voice
    data, or behavioral biometrics
  - No health, fitness, or medical data
  - No browsing history, search history, or web activity
  - No user-generated content — the Game has no chat,
    messaging, forums, leaderboards, multiplayer, or
    content sharing features
  - No cookies, web beacons, pixels, or similar tracking
    technologies
  - No telemetry from premium users — zero data of any kind
  - No data is sold, rented, leased, or traded — to
    anyone, ever, under any circumstances

3.5 IN-APP PURCHASE RECORDS

When you purchase the full game ($2.99 USD), the transaction
is processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play
Store. We do not receive, process, or store your payment
information. We receive only a boolean purchase confirmation
signal from the platform SDK to unlock premium features
locally on your device. This flag is stored in local
application storage and is never transmitted to us or any
third party.

3.6 DATA COLLECTED BY PLATFORM APP STORES

Downloads, purchases, ratings, and reviews through Apple
App Store or Google Play are governed by their respective

4. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
============================================================

We use the anonymous diagnostic data described in Section
3.2 exclusively for the following purposes:

  - Debugging and crash resolution — identifying and fixing
    software defects, crashes, and performance issues
  - Game balance and difficulty tuning — ensuring the game
    is fair and fun across all company types and difficulty
    levels
  - Compatibility testing — ensuring the game works
    correctly across different devices and OS versions
  - Feature prioritization — understanding which game
    features are used to guide development priorities
  - Aggregate health metrics — monitoring overall game
    stability (crash rates, session completion rates)

We do NOT use your data for:

  - Advertising, ad targeting, or ad personalization
  - Profiling, behavioral analysis, or segmentation of
    individual users
  - Sale, rental, licensing, or transfer to any third party
    for any reason
  - Marketing communications, email campaigns, or push
    notifications
  - Automated decision-making that produces legal or
    similarly significant effects on individuals
    (GDPR Art. 22)
  - Training machine learning models or artificial
    intelligence systems
  - Cross-app analytics, user journey mapping, or
    attribution
  - Any purpose not explicitly listed above

10. DATA SECURITY
============================================================

  - All diagnostic data is transmitted using TLS 1.2+
    encryption (HTTPS)
  - GameAnalytics maintains SOC 2 Type II compliance and
    implements industry-standard security measures including
    encryption at rest, access controls, and regular
    security audits
  - We do not store any diagnostic data on our own
    servers — we have no servers
  - The Game does not transmit data over unencrypted
    channels
  - Local save data is stored within the app sandbox,
    protected by the mobile operating system's application
    isolation (iOS App Sandbox / Android application
    sandboxing)
  - The Game does not use any custom encryption that would
    require export classification beyond EAR99

While we implement reasonable measures to protect data, no
method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.
We cannot guarantee absolute security.

============================================================

  - Notification will include: a description of the nature
    of the breach, the categories and approximate number of
