3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
============================================================

3.1 LOCAL SAVE DATA (STORED ON YOUR DEVICE ONLY)

The Game stores your game progress, settings, and preferences
locally on your device within the application sandbox
(Godot "user://" directory). This data includes:

  - Game save files (company state, employees, financials,
    game progress, quarter history)
  - User preferences (difficulty, sound settings, tutorial
    completion status)
  - Analytics consent preference
  - Age confirmation status
  - Premium unlock status

This data NEVER leaves your device. It is not uploaded to
any server, cloud service, or third party. It is accessible
only to the Game application and is deleted when you
uninstall the Game or clear its app data.

3.2 ANONYMOUS ANALYTICS DATA (FREE TRIAL USERS ONLY, WITH CONSENT)

During the free trial period, and only after you confirm you
are 13 or older and consent to data collection, we collect
anonymous, non-personally-identifiable gameplay telemetry:

  - Session data: session start/end timestamps, session
    duration
  - Progression data: quarter/year reached, game outcome
    (win/loss and reason)
  - Feature usage: screen visits, button interactions
    (hiring, fundraising, assignments, event choices)
  - Economy data: aggregate in-game cash flow patterns
    (revenue amounts, fundraising amounts, milestone bonuses)
  - Technical errors: error type, severity, and message
    (for bug fixing)
  - Device metadata: operating system, OS version, device
    model, screen resolution, app version, language/locale

Premium users (those who have purchased the full game) do
NOT have analytics collected under any circumstances. When
you purchase the full game, analytics are automatically and
permanently disabled.

3.3 INSTALLATION IDENTIFIER

When analytics are enabled, GameAnalytics (our analytics
processor) generates a random installation identifier. This
identifier:
  - Is randomly generated and unique to your app installation
  - Is NOT linked to your Apple ID, Google account, real
    identity, or any personal account
  - Cannot be used to identify you personally
  - Is deleted when raw analytics events expire (90 days)

3.4 DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

We explicitly DO NOT collect:

  - No personal identifiers (names, usernames, emails,
    phone numbers, mailing addresses)
  - No account data (no accounts, logins, or registrations
    required)
  - No financial or payment data (all purchases are handled
    entirely by the platform app stores; we never see,
    process, or store payment card numbers, bank accounts,
    or billing information)
  - No location data (no GPS, IP geolocation, cell tower
    data, or Wi-Fi location)
  - No device contents (no access to photos, contacts,
    files, calendar, camera, microphone, or device sensors)
  - No advertising identifiers (no IDFA on iOS, no GAID
    on Android)
  - No social media data or social graph information
  - No cross-app or cross-site tracking
  - No biometric data (no fingerprints, face scans, or
    voice data)
  - No health or fitness data
  - No browsing history or search history
  - No user-generated content (the Game has no chat, forums,
    or content sharing features)

3.5 IN-APP PURCHASE RECORDS

When you purchase the full game ($2.99 USD), the transaction
is processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play
Store. We do not receive, process, or store your payment
information. We only receive a purchase confirmation signal
from the platform to unlock premium features on your device.
This confirmation is stored locally on your device as a
boolean flag and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

3.6 DATA COLLECTED BY PLATFORM APP STORES

Downloads, purchases, ratings, and reviews through Apple App
Store or Google Play are subject to their respective privacy
policies, which we do not control:
  - Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  - Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

============================================================

7.1 ANALYTICS OPT-OUT (ALL USERS)

Disable analytics at any time:
  1. Open the Game
  2. Go to Settings (accessible from the main menu)
  3. Toggle "Share anonymous gameplay data" off

When disabled:
  - No new data is collected or transmitted
  - The Game functions identically — nothing is withheld
  - Previously collected data follows the retention policy
    in Section 9

7.2 DEFAULT SETTINGS

    confirmation (13+). Players must confirm age on first
    launch and are informed about data collection. Can be

Before any analytics data is collected, players are asked to
confirm they are 13 years of age or older. If a player

preferences):

    Venture > Storage > Clear Data

10. DATA SECURITY
============================================================

  - All analytics data is transmitted via TLS 1.2+
    encryption (HTTPS)
  - GameAnalytics maintains SOC 2 Type II compliance
  - Encryption at rest within GameAnalytics infrastructure
  - Access controls and regular security audits
  - No data stored on our own servers
  - No data transmitted over unencrypted channels
  - Local save data is stored within the app sandbox,
    protected by the mobile operating system's application
    isolation

While we implement reasonable measures to protect data, no
method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.
